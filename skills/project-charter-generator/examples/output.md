# Project Charter — Vendor Onboarding Redesign

_Sample data — fictional._

**Sponsor:** Dana Kim, Operations Director
**Start:** 2026-11-02
**Target completion:** 2027-02-27

## Problem

Onboarding a new vendor at Northlight Studios takes six weeks on average, with intake forms bouncing between Procurement, Legal, and Finance because no single team owns the handoff. Vendors wait, launches slip, and staff re-enter the same information three times.

## Scope

### In scope

- Redesign the vendor intake form and the handoff between Procurement, Legal, and Finance
- Single owner for each onboarding step, with a published SLA
- Pilot the redesigned flow with five new vendors

### Out of scope

- Renegotiating contracts with existing vendors
- Replacing the procurement system
- Vendor payment terms

## Success criteria

The project is done when all of these are true:

| # | Criterion | Measure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Median vendor onboarding time | From 6 weeks to 3 weeks or less |
| 2 | Intake forms completed without rework | At least 80% first-time complete |
| 3 | Pilot vendors onboarded under the new flow with no escalations past tier 1 | — |

## Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Legal review becomes a bottleneck under the new SLA | High | Pre-agreed review SLA with a named backup reviewer |
| Pilot vendors are reluctant to use the new intake form | Medium | Procurement walks the first two pilot vendors through the form live |
